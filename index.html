The Founder

Kartik Vishwakarma

Founder & Director of NP Bricks Buildcon, Kartik built the studio on a simple belief — that good construction starts with good planning, not the other way round. Every project moves through the same disciplined process: understand the site, design with intent, then build without compromise.

Based out of Lahartara, Varanasi, his team has taken projects from a blank plot to a finished home or commercial space, handling architecture, interiors, exteriors and site execution as one continuous service rather than handoffs between contractors.
